About Us

Background story: The idea was created by Thabo Baloyi and Sipho Ntwana and established in 2020 in Johannesburg to give underground brands a platform. TS HUB SA can represent more than 50 brands in one store.

Mission statement: To provide a modern online marketplace where customers can discover, shop, and support upcoming fashion brands through a secure, affordable, and user-friendly shopping experience.

Vision statement: To become South Africa's leading online marketplace for emerging streetwear and fashion.
